# Indian Community in Finland

A reference list of Indian community organisations in Finland — kept as a plain doc, not
wired into the app, per the same convention as [TRIVIA.md](TRIVIA.md) and
[RESOURCES.md](RESOURCES.md). These are **self-organised community groups, not official
government advice** — the one exception is the Embassy of India itself, listed separately
below.

**Not exhaustive.** The Embassy of India's own bilateral brief counted 16 registered Indian
associations in Finland as of February 2024, and a June 2025 Embassy event involved
representatives from over 20 — so more groups exist than are listed here. Every link below
was checked reachable before being added; anything that only turned up as a "search Facebook
for X" lead was deliberately left out rather than guessed, matching this repo's no-invented-
URLs rule. If you're looking for a specific regional or language community not listed here,
the Embassy is the best place to ask.

**Overlap note:** several of these organisations are already used elsewhere in this repo —
Maharashtra Mandal Finland, Gujarati Samaj Finland, and Bengali Association of Finland appear
in the app's own "Community Events" popup (`COMMUNITY_EVENTS` in `js/data.js`), and Indian
Women in Finland / Bharatvaasi / Finland-India Society appear in the app's "Resources" popup.
This page is the fuller reference list, not a replacement for either.

## Pan-Indian & women's communities

| Organisation | Website | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Suomi-Intia-Seura (Finnish-Indian Society) | https://suomiintiaseura.fi/ | Pan-Indian friendship society, founded 1949 — organises Gandhi Jayanti, Diwali, and other events. |
| Bharatvaasi in Finland | https://www.bharatvaasi.fi/ | Community platform and network for Indians living in Finland. |
| Indian Women in Finland (IWF Ry) | https://www.iwf.fi/ | Networking, mentoring, and events for Indian women in Finland. |

## Regional & language communities

| Organisation | Website | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Maharashtra Mandal Finland | https://mmfinland.com/ | Marathi community — runs Ganesh Chaturthi (Ganpati) and other Marathi cultural events. |
| Gujarati Samaj Finland | https://www.gujaratisamaj.fi/ | Gujarati community association, established 2018 — runs the annual Navratri Raas Garba festival. |
| Finland Telugu Association (FiTA) | https://www.fintelugu.org/ | Non-profit for Telugu speakers, founded 2012. |
| Finland Tamil Sangam (FINTA) | https://www.finlandtamilsangam.com/ | Community association for Tamil speakers in Finland. |
| Bengali Association of Finland (BAFIN/BAF) | https://bafin.fi/ | Has run Durga Puja in the capital region every year since 1999. |

## Events & official

| Organisation | Website | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| India Day / Intia-päivä | https://blog.intiapaiva.fi/ | Annual pan-Indian cultural event in Helsinki, run with the Finnish-Indian Society and Embassy of India — 9th edition held in 2025. |
| Embassy of India, Helsinki | https://www.indembhelsinki.gov.in/ | Official government contact — the best place to verify associations not listed here. |

## Further associations reported to exist (unverified links — name only)

The Embassy's association count, and an older community directory, reference additional
regional/language and religious organisations that we could not find a verifiable, currently
live website for: **Finland Kannada Association (FINKA)**, **Finland Malayalee Association /
FIMA**, **World Malayalee Federation Finland**, **Maharashtra**-adjacent **Punjab Cultural
Society Finland**, **Finland Hindi Association**, **Bihar Jharkhand Parishad Finland**,
**Odia Association Finland**, **All India Cultural Association**, and Sikh gurdwaras in
**Helsinki** and **Vantaa**. If you're part of one of these and have a current website or
social page, it's worth adding here with a real link rather than a search suggestion.
