# Fun Facts About Finland

A few lighter things worth knowing before you land — some of these genuinely help explain
local behaviour you'll notice in your first few weeks (the sauna one especially).

* **Reindeer vs. Humans in Lapland.** Across Finland as a whole, humans win by a wide margin
  (around 5.6 million people versus 200,000 reindeer). But in **Lapland** (northern Finland),
  reindeer actually outnumber the human population.
* **The Salmiakki Secret.** The sharp, salty kick in *salmiakki* (Finland's iconic salty
  liquorice) doesn't come from regular table salt — it comes from **ammonium chloride**
  (*salmiak salt*), an ingredient that started life as a 19th-century cough medicine and chest
  expectorant before it became candy.
* **Coffee Champions.** Finns drink more coffee per capita than any other nation on earth —
  roughly **12 kilograms of light-roast coffee per person per year**, about 4–8 cups a day.
* **More Saunas Than Cars.** Finland has roughly 3 million saunas for a population of 5.6
  million — enough total sauna space to fit the entire country inside at once.
* **Oddball World Championships.** Finland hosts some wonderfully eccentric global
  competitions, including the **Wife-Carrying World Championships**, **Swamp Soccer**, and
  **Heavy Metal Knitting**.
* **Heavy Metal Capital.** Finland has the highest concentration of heavy metal bands per
  capita in the world — over **53 bands per 100,000 residents**.
* **The Land Is Rising.** Finland's coastline is still rebounding from the weight of Ice Age
  glaciers (glacial rebound) and grows by roughly **7 square kilometers every year**.
* **Everyman's Right (*Jokamiehenoikeus*).** Public access laws give anyone the legal right to
  roam freely, pitch a tent, and pick wild berries and mushrooms across almost all forests and
  wilderness, regardless of who owns the land.
* **No Cap on Speeding Fines.** Traffic fines are calculated from the offender's income
  (*day-fines*) — high earners have occasionally received speeding tickets worth over
  **€100,000**.
* **National Day of Failure.** Every **October 13th**, Finland celebrates the *National Day
  of Failure*, encouraging risk-taking and destigmatizing setbacks in business and life.
