# Additional Resources for Relocating from India to Finland

A reference directory of real websites for people moving from India to Finland — kept
separate from the app's quest-generation logic (`js/data.js`) because these are general
reference material, not personalized quest steps. All URLs below are used exactly as
provided; none were invented.

**Websites change.** Treat every entry here as a pointer to verify against, not as a
guarantee the content or URL hasn't moved — the app's own `js/data.js` file carries the same
caveat for the sources it cites in generated quests.

**Overlap note:** InfoFinland, Migri, DVV, Kela, Vero, and TE-palvelut are already used
throughout the app as specific per-quest citations (see `SOURCES` in `js/data.js`) — those
entries link to precise sub-pages relevant to one quest. The entries below are each
institution's general/homepage or a different specific page, kept for browsing rather than
duplicating the exact quest citations.

## Part 1 — Official Government & Authority Websites

Official sources should be your primary reference for anything legally or financially
binding: immigration, residence permits, taxes, social security, healthcare eligibility,
driving licence rules, and government registration.

### Immigration & Residence Permits

People move to Finland for work, study, family reunification, business, or other reasons,
and residence permit requirements differ depending on the reason. Migri is the primary
official authority for Finnish residence permit and immigration matters. Students, employees,
and family members can face different application processes. Always verify current
requirements directly through official Migri information — rules change.

| Name | URL | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Finnish Immigration Service (Migri) | https://migri.fi/en | Official Finnish immigration authority — residence permits, work, studies, family reunification, permanent residence. |
| Migri — Residence Permits | https://migri.fi/en/applying-for-a-residence-permit | Official information about applying for Finnish residence permits. |

### Driving & Transport (official)

Driving licence rules for people arriving with a foreign licence — including an Indian
licence — depend on individual circumstances. **Do not assume an Indian driving licence is
either always valid or always invalid in Finland** — check current official guidance
directly through Traficom before relying on any summary, including this one.

| Name | URL | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Traficom | https://www.traficom.fi/en | Finnish Transport and Communications Agency — transport, vehicles, driving, and driving licence regulations. |
| Ajovarma | https://www.ajovarma.fi/en/ | Driving licence services, driving tests, and related practical services. |

### Finnish Weather & Winter

Cold winters, snow, and ice call for layered clothing and proper winter boots; darkness is
pronounced in winter and daylight hours are very long in summer. Walking safely on icy
surfaces and appropriate indoor heating habits are both worth learning early.

| Name | URL | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Finnish Meteorological Institute | https://en.ilmatieteenlaitos.fi/ | Official weather forecasts and information about Finnish weather conditions. |

### Indian Community in Finland

Community and language-based groups (Facebook, WhatsApp, Instagram, cultural associations,
student organizations) are usually the fastest way to find people with shared background —
but they aren't centrally listed anywhere official, and specific group links weren't
independently verified for this list, so we've limited this section to established
organizations with public websites. Labeled clearly as community resources, not government
advice.

### Hobbies, Clubs & Activities

No single national directory covers sports clubs, children's activities, music, dance,
hiking, photography, coding, arts, or board games — the most reliable path is usually your
**local city website** (see City Information above), local libraries, community centres,
student organizations, and local hobby groups specific to where you land.
